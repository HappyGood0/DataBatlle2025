--- Question 1 (Chunk 005) ---
 Question 1 : What is the essential requirement for an invention to be patentable under the European Patent Convention (EPC)?

    A) The invention must be a mathematical method.
    B) The invention must be a business method.
    C) The invention must have a technical character or technicality.
    D) The invention must be a computer-implemented method.

Correct answer: C

Explanation: According to the text, an invention may be an invention within the meaning of Article 52(1) EPC if it has a technical character or technicality. Therefore, the essential requirement for an invention to be patentable under the EPC is that it has a technical character or technicality.

Source: Article 52(1) EPC 1973 and the text provided.

Question 1: Which of the following is a requirement for a solution to be considered non-obvious under the problem-solution approach?

    A) The solution must be disclosed in the application as providing the solution to the problem.
    B) The solution must not be disclosed in the application as providing the solution to the problem.
    C) The solution must not have the alleged effect.
    D) The solution must be disclosed in the prior art.

Correct answer: A

Explanation: According to the text, a solution claimed as non-obvious is patentable only if it actually has the alleged effect. This means that the solution must be disclosed in the application as providing the solution to the problem.
