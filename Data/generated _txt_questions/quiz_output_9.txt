--- Question 19 (Chunk 327) ---
1. Which of the following activities is not considered as professional activity for the purpose of the EQE enrolment?

    A) Representing an employer before the EPO as a patent attorney.
    B) Working as an employee in the field of national or international applications and patents.
    C) Being formally authorized to act as a patent attorney by an employer.
    D) Acting as a patent attorney for an employer but not being authorized to do so.

Correct answer: D

Explanation: According to the text, professional activity can be recognized only if and for such time as candidates were able to represent their employer before the EPO and in fact did so. They must have been authorized to act as representative by their employer, but formal authorization is not enough by itself, as it does not show whether, as required, they have actually worked in that capacity ( D 1/13). Therefore, acting as a patent attorney for an employer but not being authorized to do so is not considered as professional activity for the purpose of the EQE enrolment.

Source: Article 11(2)(a)(ii) REE, D 1/13.
