--- Question 1 (Chunk 167) ---

    A) A witness is put forward to substantiate facts of which he/she has personal knowledge, while an expert is put forward to provide an opinion on a technical matter.
    B) A witness is put forward to provide an opinion on a technical matter, while an expert is put forward to substantiate facts of which he/she has personal knowledge.
