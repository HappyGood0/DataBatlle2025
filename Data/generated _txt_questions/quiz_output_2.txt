--- Question 2 (Chunk 052) ---
1. Which of the following is a reason for a computer program product to not be excluded from patentability under Article 52(2) and (3) EPC 1973?

    A) The computer program product does not produce a further technical effect.
    B) The computer program product is not run on a computer.
    C) The computer program product is not described in mathematical terms.
    D) The computer program product produces a technical effect that is known in the prior art.

Correct answer: A

Explanation: According to the text, a computer program product is not excluded from patentability under Article 52(2) and (3) EPC 1973 if, when it is run on a computer, it produces a further technical effect that goes beyond the "normal" physical interactions between program (software) and computer (hardware). Therefore, a computer program product that does not produce a further technical effect would be excluded from patentability.

Source: T 1173/97 (OJ 1999, 609)

--- Question 6 (Chunk 056) ---

--- Question 9 (Chunk 059) ---
 Question 1 : Which of the following is a reason for excluding a feature from being considered in the assessment of inventive step of a combination of features?

    A) The feature contributes causally to the solution of the problem.
    B) The feature is a technical non-functional modification.
    C) The feature is not described in the description.
    D) The feature does not provide a surprising advantage.

Correct answer: D

Explanation: According to the text, a feature is only to be considered in the assessment of inventive step if it contributes causally to the solution of the problem. Therefore, a feature that does not provide a surprising advantage is not a reason for excluding it from being considered. However, if a feature does not contribute causally to the solution of the problem, it is not relevant for the assessment of inventive step.

Source: I.D.9. Assessment of inventive step, T 37/82, T 65/87, T 144/90, T 206/91, T 226/94, T 912/94
