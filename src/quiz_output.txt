1. What is the difference between the pre-examination and the European qualifying examination (EQE)?

Question: What is the difference between the pre-examination and the European qualifying examination (EQE)?

A. The pre-examination is a multiple-choice examination while the EQE is an essay examination.
B. The pre-examination is a timed exam while the EQE is not timed.
C. The pre-examination is a mandatory exam while the EQE is an optional exam.
D. The pre-examination is a closed-book exam while the EQE is an open-book exam.

Correct answer: A. The pre-examination is a multiple-choice examination while the EQE is an essay examination.

Explanation: According to the text, the pre-examination is a multiple-choice examination, while the European qualifying examination (EQE) is an essay examination.
